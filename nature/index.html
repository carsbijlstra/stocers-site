Nature provides. gives back.

Our firelighters are made of wood wool and wax, so nature is our raw material. That is a responsibility, not a slogan. Together with our dealers and everyone who lights a STOCERS fire, we help plant at least 200 trees every year.

01 Natural material

Wood and wax, nothing else

Fine wood wool dipped in wax. No chemicals, no fillers, neutral in scent. What comes from the forest goes back to the forest.

02 Every year

At least 200 new trees

A share of every pallet we sell goes to tree planting. Each year we fund a minimum of 200 trees, and we grow that number as we grow.

03 Open about it

Counted, not guessed

We report what was actually planted, where, and when. This page is where you can follow it, season after season.

The pledge

Every fire we help start, gives a tree back.

Fire is warmth, food and time spent together. It should not cost the forest that makes it possible. That is why giving back is part of the product, not an extra.

Growing impact

Follow the trees we plant

This page grows with the project. Planting numbers, locations and photos from the field will be published here as each season is completed. Are you a dealer and would you like to see the impact of your own volume, or add your name to a planting? Get in touch.
